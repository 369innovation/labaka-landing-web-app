Our Story

Born from a simple question — why is it so hard to connect?

Every day, thousands of interesting people pass each other by. At events, on streets, in cafés — potential friendships, collaborations, and communities that never happen simply because there was no bridge.

We asked: what if your phone could be that bridge? Not in a cold, algorithmic way — but in a way that felt human, warm, and real. That question became Labaka.

Labaka isn't just an app. It's the belief that every person you haven't met yet is a connection waiting to happen.

— The Labaka Team
