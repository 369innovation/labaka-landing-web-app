Section 03 — New

Photo Data & Profile Image Usage NEW

3.1 How We Use Your Photos

Profile photos you upload to Labaka are displayed to other users as part of your profile in Nearby, Global Discovery, and Explore features. Beyond display, Labaka may analyze engagement data associated with your profile photos — such as which photo receives more views, taps, or connection requests — to help optimize your profile's presentation and ordering within the app. This analysis is conducted to improve your experience on the platform.

3.2 Photo Ordering & Optimization

Based on engagement data, Labaka may suggest or automatically reorder your profile photos to improve your visibility and connection rate. You retain full control over your photos at all times and can manually reorder, add, or remove photos from your profile through the profile settings. You may opt out of automatic photo ordering at any time through Settings → Profile → Photo Preferences.

3.3 Photo Data Limitations

Labaka does not use facial recognition technology on your photos. Photo engagement data is used solely for profile optimization purposes within the platform and is not shared with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes. Photo data is deleted along with your account upon account deletion.

Your Control: You can add, remove, or reorder your profile photos at any time. Opting out of photo optimization does not affect your ability to use any other Labaka feature.

Section 04

Why We Collect Your Data

4.1 Lawful Purposes

We collect and process your personal data only for the following lawful purposes:

  • To provide the service — account creation, profile display, connections, chat, video calls, stories, posts, events, offers, and opportunity features
  • To personalize your experience — showing relevant nearby people, events, and opportunities based on your location, mode, and preferences
  • To optimize your profile — analyzing photo engagement to improve your profile's presentation and visibility
  • To enable payments — processing LBK coin purchases, subscription billing, and transaction history
  • To ensure safety — detecting prohibited content, fraud, fake accounts, and policy violations through both automated and human moderation
  • To improve the platform — analytics, feature development, and performance monitoring
  • To comply with law — responding to legal obligations, court orders, or law enforcement requests under Indian law
  • To communicate with you — service notifications, policy updates, support responses, and push notifications

Our Commitment: We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties for commercial gain. Your data is used solely to operate and improve Labaka.

Section 05

Location Data & Real-Time Nearby

5.1 How We Use Location

Labaka's core features — Real-Time Nearby People, Global Discovery, Explore Nearby, Events, and Offers — depend on your device location. We collect precise GPS location when the app is open and in use. Labaka is a location-based application and location access is integral to its core functionality. We do not collect location data when the app is closed unless you have explicitly enabled background location in your device settings.

5.2 Location Visibility to Other Users

Your approximate location — not exact GPS coordinates — is displayed to other users through Nearby People and Explore Nearby features. Exact coordinates are never shared with other users. By using location-based features, you acknowledge and accept that your approximate proximity will be visible to nearby Labaka users.

5.3 Location Data Retention

Real-time location data used for Nearby features is not permanently stored. It is used transiently to generate proximity results and is discarded after each session. Aggregated, non-identifiable location analytics may be retained for platform improvement purposes only.

Important: While we protect your precise location, enabling Real-Time Nearby means your approximate location is visible to other Labaka users in your vicinity. Exercise personal judgment about your usage of location-based features at all times.

Section 06

Chat, Video & Communications Data

6.1 Chat Data

Messages exchanged via Labaka's chat feature are stored on our secure servers to deliver the chat experience and enable message history. Chat data is not routinely accessed by Labaka staff. When a report is submitted, relevant chat records may be reviewed by our Trust & Safety team for investigation purposes only.

6.2 Video Calls

Video calls on Labaka are transmitted in real-time and are not recorded or stored by Labaka. We do not retain video call content. Call metadata — duration, timestamp, and participants — may be logged for technical and safety purposes only.

6.3 Trust & Safety Review

By using Labaka's communication features, you acknowledge and consent that in the event of a reported violation, Labaka's Trust & Safety team may access relevant communications strictly for investigation and enforcement purposes. This access is limited, purpose-bound, and logged internally.

Section 07 — New

Content Moderation NEW

7.1 Automated Moderation

Labaka uses automated moderation tools — including AI-based content detection systems — to proactively identify and flag prohibited content across the platform. This includes automated scanning of profile photos, story content, post listings, and public-facing text for nudity, hate speech, spam, and other prohibited content as defined in our Terms & Conditions. Automated moderation operates continuously and does not require a user report to be triggered.

7.2 Human Moderation

In addition to automated tools, Labaka employs human Trust & Safety reviewers who may review content flagged by automated systems or reported by users. Human review is conducted for the sole purpose of enforcing Labaka's Community Standards and Terms & Conditions. Reviewers are bound by strict confidentiality obligations and may only access content in the context of an active moderation review.

7.3 Moderation of Private Communications

Private chat messages are not subject to routine proactive automated scanning. However, where a chat is reported by a participant for a violation, the relevant messages may be reviewed by our Trust & Safety team. By using Labaka's chat feature, you consent to this limited, report-triggered review.

Why We Moderate: Proactive moderation of public-facing content — profiles, stories, posts, and listings — is how we keep Labaka safe for all users. This is standard practice across leading social platforms and is done in full compliance with applicable Indian law.

Section 08 — New

Content Visibility After Deletion NEW

8.1 Platform Removal vs. User Copies

When you delete a story, post, opportunity listing, profile photo, or any other content from Labaka, that content is removed from public view on our platform promptly. However, Labaka cannot guarantee or control whether other users have already captured, screenshotted, copied, downloaded, or otherwise retained your content before its deletion.

Once another user has viewed your content and saved a copy — through any means including screenshots, screen recordings, or external downloads — that copy exists outside of Labaka's control. Labaka is not responsible for the continued existence, sharing, or redistribution of any such copied content.

8.2 Stories Specific Notice

Stories are particularly subject to this limitation. Because stories are actively viewed by connected users during their active period, the risk of capture is higher than with other content types. Users are strongly advised to exercise discretion in what they post to Stories, understanding that Labaka cannot prevent or reverse the capture of story content by viewers during its active period.

8.3 Profile Content After Account Deletion

Upon account deletion, your Labaka profile is removed from the platform immediately. However, other users who previously viewed, connected with, or saved information from your profile may retain copies of that information. Labaka is not liable for any third-party retention of your profile information that occurred prior to your account deletion.

Think before you post: Once content is viewed by others on Labaka, it may exist beyond your control. Labaka strongly encourages all users to share only content they are comfortable with being permanently retained by others, regardless of deletion.

Section 09

LBK Wallet & Payment Data

9.1 What We Store

We store your LBK coin balance, coin transaction history, reward history, and subscription plan details. We do not store full payment card numbers, CVV codes, or full bank account details. All financial transactions are processed by third-party payment processors who maintain their own security and compliance standards.

9.2 Payment Processor Data

When you make a purchase, your payment details are submitted directly to our third-party payment processor. Labaka receives only a transaction confirmation and tokenized payment reference. Your full financial data is governed by the payment processor's own privacy policy.

9.3 Fraud Prevention

Transaction data may be used to detect and prevent fraudulent activity, abuse of the LBK coin system, or manipulation of rewards. Accounts found abusing the rewards system may be suspended and coin balances forfeited.

Section 10

Stories, Posts & Opportunity Data

10.1 Stories

Story content is stored temporarily and made visible to your connected and super-liked users for its active duration. Stories are deleted from our servers after expiry. However, Labaka cannot control whether other users have captured your story content during its active period. See Section 08 for full details on content visibility after deletion.

10.2 Opportunity Posts & Content

Content you post in the Opportunity feature is stored on our servers and may be visible to other users based on your location and settings. You may delete your own posts at any time through the app. Deleted post data is removed from public view immediately and purged from our servers within 30 days.

10.3 Reported Content

Content that has been reported for a violation may be preserved beyond normal retention periods for the purpose of investigating the report and cooperating with law enforcement if required. This preservation is limited to the specific content flagged in the report.

Section 11

How We Share Your Data

11.1 With Other Users

Your profile information — including name, photo, bio, interests, profession, and mode — is visible to other Labaka users as part of the discovery and connection experience. Your exact location is never shared; only your approximate proximity is shown in Nearby features.

11.2 With Service Providers

We share data with trusted third-party service providers including cloud hosting providers, payment processors, analytics services, moderation tools, and customer support tools. All service providers are bound by data processing agreements restricting use of your data to the specific stated purpose.

11.3 With Law Enforcement

We may disclose your personal data to law enforcement agencies, courts, or government authorities when required by applicable Indian law, court order, or legal process — including the IT Act, 2000, the DPDP Act, 2023, and POCSO, 2012. We will notify you of such disclosures where legally permitted.

11.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of Labaka's business, your personal data may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you prior to such transfer and your continued use constitutes consent to the transfer.

We Do NOT: Sell your personal data to advertisers · Share your data with third parties for their own marketing · Disclose your precise location to other users · Share your chat content with anyone except in response to a valid legal request or reported violation investigation.

12.2 Inactive Account Policy NEW

If your Labaka account remains inactive — meaning you have not logged in or used the platform in any way — for a continuous period of 4 years, Labaka reserves the right to initiate an account deletion process. We will attempt to notify you via your registered email address at least 30 days before initiating deletion. If no response is received and no login activity occurs within that 30-day notice period, your account and associated data will be permanently deleted in accordance with this Policy.

You may prevent account deletion due to inactivity simply by logging into the app before the end of the 4-year inactivity period. If you wish to retain your account without regular use, we recommend logging in at least once annually.

12.3 Support Communication Records NEW

All communications between you and Labaka's support team — including emails, in-app support messages, grievance submissions, and appeal records — are retained for a period of 6 years from the date of the interaction. This retention is maintained for legal compliance, audit purposes, dispute resolution, and to provide continuity of support history. Support records are stored securely and are not shared with other users or third parties except where required by law.

12.4 Account Deletion

When you delete your Labaka account, your public profile and content are removed from the platform immediately. Residual data is purged from our servers within 90 days, except where retention is required by law or for ongoing investigations. Transaction data is retained for the statutory period required under Indian financial law.

Section 14

Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

14.1 Your Data Rights

As a Data Principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

Right to Access

Request a summary of personal data we hold about you and how it is being processed.

Right to Correction

Request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal data.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the stated purpose.

Right to Grievance

Lodge a grievance with our Grievance Officer and receive a response within the prescribed period.

Right to Nominate

Nominate an individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent for data processing at any time, noting that this may limit platform features.

14.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

Contact our Grievance Officer at laabaakaa@gmail.com with your registered email and specific request. We will respond within 30 days as required under the DPDP Act, 2023. You also have the right to escalate unresolved grievances to the Data Protection Board of India.

Section 15 — New

Banned Accounts & Data NEW

15.1 Data Retention Post-Ban

When an account is permanently terminated by Labaka for a violation of our Terms & Conditions, we may retain certain data associated with that account — including the registered phone number, email address, device identifiers, and IP addresses used — for the purpose of preventing that user from creating new accounts on the platform.

15.2 Ban Enforcement Across New Accounts

Labaka uses retained identifiers from banned accounts to detect and block ban circumvention attempts. If a banned user attempts to create a new Labaka account using different credentials but the same device, phone number, IP address, or other identifiable signals, Labaka may terminate the new account immediately. The creation of a new account following a ban is itself a violation of our Terms and justifies immediate re-termination without appeal.

15.3 Violation Evidence Retention

Where a termination was based on a serious violation — including but not limited to sexually explicit content, conduct involving minors, fraud, or incitement of violence — Labaka may retain the specific content or communication evidence that formed the basis of the termination for a period necessary to cooperate with any law enforcement investigation or legal proceedings, regardless of our standard retention periods.

Note: Retention of banned account identifiers is conducted solely for platform safety and abuse prevention purposes and is not used for any commercial or advertising purpose.
